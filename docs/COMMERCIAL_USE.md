# Commercial Use

ScrapHeads Time Tracker was created to help nonprofit robotics teams manage student hours, attendance, and team participation.

Companies may not sell, rebrand, host, or package this app as their own product without written permission.

Examples of commercial use requiring permission include:

- selling the app;
- offering it as a paid SaaS product;
- hosting it for teams for a fee;
- including it in a paid consulting package;
- using it as part of a commercial robotics management platform;
- removing ScrapHeads attribution and presenting it as a company-owned product.
